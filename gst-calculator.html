ℹ️ GST Information

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is an indirect tax applied to the supply of goods and services in India. This calculator helps you quickly add or remove GST from any amount.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is GST?
GST (Goods and Services Tax) is an indirect tax charged on most goods and services in India.


2. What is the difference between Add GST and Remove GST?
Add GST calculates the tax on an amount before GST. Remove GST extracts the GST from an amount that already includes GST.


3. Which GST rates are commonly used?
The most common GST rates are 3%, 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%, depending on the product or service.


4. Can I enter a custom GST rate?
Yes. Select Custom from the GST Rate dropdown and enter any GST percentage.
