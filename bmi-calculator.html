❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What is a healthy BMI?
A healthy BMI for most adults is between 18.5 and 24.9.


Q. Can BMI measure body fat?
No. BMI is only an estimate based on height and weight. It does not directly measure body fat.


Q. Should athletes use BMI?
Athletes may have a high BMI because of muscle mass, so BMI may not always reflect their health.


Q. How often should I check BMI?
Checking once every few weeks is usually enough to monitor progress.
