📘 BMI Information

BMI (Body Mass Index) is calculated using your height and weight. It helps estimate whether your weight falls within a healthy range. BMI is a useful screening tool but should not replace medical advice.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is BMI?
BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple calculation using your height and weight to estimate whether your weight is healthy.


2. What is a healthy BMI?
For most adults, a healthy BMI is between 18.5 and 24.9.


3. Is BMI accurate?
BMI is a useful screening tool but it does not directly measure body fat. Athletes and muscular people may have a higher BMI without having excess body fat.


4. How often should I calculate my BMI?
Checking every few weeks or once a month is usually enough to monitor your progress.
