📘 Unit Information

A unit converter changes a measurement from one unit to another while keeping the same physical quantity.

This converter supports common units for length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time and data storage.

The conversion is performed automatically using the appropriate conversion factor or temperature formula.
