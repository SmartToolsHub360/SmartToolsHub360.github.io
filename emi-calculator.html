ℹ️ EMI Information

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay your loan. Each EMI includes both the principal amount and the interest charged by the lender.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is EMI?
EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed monthly payment made towards your loan.


2. What affects EMI?
Loan Amount, Interest Rate and Loan Tenure directly affect your monthly EMI.


3. Can I reduce my EMI?
Yes. A lower interest rate or a longer tenure reduces your monthly EMI.
